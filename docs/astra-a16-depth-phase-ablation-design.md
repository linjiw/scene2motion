**Status: design only; not preregistered, prepared or launched.** September 6, 2026.

## Fixed diagnostic intervention

Evaluate the existing A12 `no_phase` references, without regeneration, refitting
or threshold changes. Compare nominal, fixed shallow, full correction, and this
depth-only ablation on all12 A15 scene/u0 units. For the two overhead-deficit
branches, no-phase keeps depth0.30 m and restores onset lead1.08 m while retaining
recovery offset1.38 m. This is a previously specified ablation, not a new command
tuned to A15's outcomes. It tests the incremental onset edit at the deeper depth;
it does not by itself identify a full depth-by-onset interaction.

Use the identical original A12 nominal32-reference backgrounds, slots, modes and
physics seed. Compile one target at a time. Before any outcome, match the full
compiled payload and complete simulator/source context against A15's verified
executions. Reuse only exact identities with explicit alias receipts; never cache
by target reference alone. Expect the other ten ablation requests to alias prior
nominal/shallow contexts, but byte and context checks must establish this before
the final executable budget is set. Keep all12 rows in the analysis.

## Evidence and decision boundary

Report raw and original baseline-preserving selections, whole-body margin,
rear-body exit, one-second continuation, tracking termination and refusals.
Keep A15's unchanged40mm margin and0.45 m continuation criterion; the0.426 m
full-correction result remains a failure. No present outcome may choose a command.

This follow-up uses the same development units after seeing A15. A gain is a
mechanism lead requiring validation on the remaining reused development seeds
under a separately frozen protocol, then untouched confirmation. It cannot
retroactively pass A15, establish statistical significance, or support a physical
traversal claim. A tie/loss rules out this simplification as a useful successor
on this pilot; do not iterate timing/depth offsets on these units.

Before launch, commit an executable protocol with exact source hashes, all12
rows, alias ledger, complete execution-context comparison, nominal-control reuse
requirements, job budget and the unchanged compact resource/stop rules. If reuse
cannot be proven, stop and assign explicit fresh controls rather than silently
treating cached previews as current. No simulation is assigned by this design.

After this bounded ablation, a new correction needs evidence about achievable
clearance and progress jointly. Contact sensitivity and a paired obstacle-present
pilot of the strongest qualified compiler remain necessary before broader beam,
multi-beam and stepping performance claims. No additional inverse-response model,
QP or downstream learner is justified merely by this failed promotion.
