Clear the beam—and keep moving.

Native motion controls can produce local beam passage with frozen ARDY-G1 and SONIC. Our latest experiments ask whether execution-aware correction improves on a simple shallow duck. Depth-only correction gained one pilot case in A16, but A17 found no additional gain on the remaining development comparison. We retain the negative result and close this correction rule before expanding the benchmark.

The system we are testing

  1. Describe the obstacle-relative motion

    Known beam geometry and a forward route set native root-height constraints. Depth, onset lead and recovery offset control the duck. ARDY-G1 produces the motion with frozen weights.

  2. Measure the actual tracked preview

    SONIC tracks with the obstacle absent. Replay achieved whole-body states against the beam and measure clearance, exit time and continued forward motion. Keep one target in a fixed 31-reference background.

  3. Correct once, verify, select or abstain

    Preserve a qualified nominal preview. Otherwise test one bounded native correction and measure that exact candidate. A selected preview still needs an obstacle-present evaluation.

This is offline compilation using known geometry and simulated state. It does not impose a hard velocity constraint on the frozen controller. The common background makes comparisons controlled; it does not establish simulator independence. One useful target currently costs a 32-environment job.

What changed in the recent experiments?

1 · Better candidate supply, limited prediction

A10 tested smaller native edits across 12 reused scenes and 48 units. Fixed shallow qualified {{A10_RAW}} / 48 raw candidates, versus 7 / 48 nominal. The predictor still failed its readiness checks. A12's full correction selected 13 / 48, below shallow's 15 / 48.

Why the full correction failed

2 · Make the comparison interpretable

Identical target references could track differently when other batch inputs changed. A13 localized an observed early divergence downstream of equal applied actions. A14's fixed-background adapter then reproduced all 64 paired execution prefixes exactly.

Controlled-context validation

3 · A small depth-only lead

With the common context, A15 full correction tied shallow at 4 / 12. A16 removed the phase edit and reached 5 / 12. On the gained case, it traded 4.29 mm of clearance for 75.23 mm of continuation and crossed the existing progress gate.

A16 result and mechanism

4 · The lead did not extend

A17 tested the only remaining pair that could distinguish depth-only from shallow. Both failed qualification. The preregistered stop rule avoided 35 further jobs while preserving unknown absolute outcomes.

What would make this a complete robotics paper?

The technical question is now precise: can a bounded native motion compiler improve obstacle-present clearance and continued motion beyond fixed shallow and equally budgeted search? The strongest current contribution is the measured failure structure and a controlled evaluation procedure. General correction superiority remains open.

Established obstacle-present baseline: A5, with its original endpoint

A5 generated {{REFERENCES}} references and verified {{EXECUTIONS}} absent/present operational executions across three holds and three encoder modes. Fixed3/default completed 17 / 32 local passages, from eight motion-seed groups and four slot repeats on one reused beam geometry. All 17 exceed 25 cm of continuation in the later diagnostic. Full-route success is 0 / 32.

Operational local passage requires the rear body envelope beyond x=1.32 m, root beyond x=1.82 m, upright and inside the corridor for 0.20 s by 4.0 s, with no nominal or 40 mm-inflated geometric hazard in that prefix.
