# Paper direction: measured native control under frozen generation and tracking

The target contribution is a useful native height–forward-timing parameterization,
a query-efficient execution-conditioned compiler, and prospective obstacle-present
passage with continuation. Only the first component is implemented so far. A19 is
candidate discovery; no learned rule or new traversal improvement is established.

The operational bottleneck is joint clearance and continuation. A16 and A17 supply
individual examples of a tradeoff, not a universal response law. A17's35 equivalent
selected pairs restrict what its particular depth-only rule could change. They do
not close native correction. A5 fixed3/default's17/32 local passages remain the
physical-simulation starting point; full-route completion was0/32.

## Evidence required before each claim

|---|---|---|---|
| Timing adds useful candidate supply | Nine joint commands vs beta=0 depths and exact nominal/shallow | Eight fresh scenes, two carriers each; achieved clearance plus complete1s continuation | A19 preregistered; outcome pending |
| New candidate capability survives the obstacle | Absent-selected joint vs shallow and fixed3/default | All16 assigned units; at most48 target executions; abstentions fail completion | Conditional bridge, unassigned |
| Execution feedback chooses commands efficiently | Two-preview correction vs best fixed joint and matched-space search | Scene-held-out development, then six fresh pilot scenes×two seeds | No predictor fitted |
| Frozen compiler generalizes | Paired strongest simple/search controls | Proposed24 new scene groups×three seeds; scene-level paired uncertainty | Confirmation unassigned |
| Passage preserves useful route state | Uninterrupted two-beam routes | Local passages and complete route, all route previews charged | Extension only |

A19's gate requires timing-dependent qualification beyond all beta=0 depths and
both historical comparators on at least two independent scenes. This conservative
engineering gate is frozen before outcomes. A pass permits a present capability
bridge, not a two-preview superiority claim. A failure blocks predictor fitting
for this design; retain the continuous response and diagnose supply/aliasing.

A practical compiler should preserve a qualified nominal preview, propose one
bounded joint edit, preview that exact output and otherwise abstain. A fixed-shallow
fallback is a second request when chosen; it is not a third free measurement.
Report paired losses as well as gains against shallow. Train/validate response
models by whole scene, beginning with interpolation or a small regularized model.

## Novelty boundary and final presentation

[ARDY](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/ardy/) already supplies native
kinematic constraints and integration with SONIC on G1. Integration itself is not
our contribution. [CLoSD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03441) already couples motion
generation with physics feedback. Generic feedback-based editing is too broad.
[Learning Whole-Body Humanoid Locomotion via Motion Generation and Motion Tracking](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17335v2)
uses a trained terrain-conditioned generator and tracking policy, including tracker
fine-tuning with the generator frozen, and reports real G1 terrain traversal.
Our proposed intervention regime freezes both released generator and tracker and
measures what a small native-command interface can accomplish at a declared query
cost. That resource constraint becomes a contribution only with useful outcomes.

Organize a successful paper around three figures: obstacle-present paired outcomes;
requested-to-achieved clearance–continuation responses; held-out success versus
preview cost. A second absent-to-present bridge figure should expose selected
candidates that fail with the physical obstacle. Rejected-candidate false negatives
need separately assigned present diagnostics; they cannot be inferred from selected
executions. Keep historic audits as motivation and supporting apparatus evidence.

A18 ended at its force-sensitivity gate: points32/32, nonzero reported impulse0/32,
exact logging/no-logging states, four jobs unlaunched. Whole-body force detection is
unvalidated. Preserve nominal penetration, inflated-margin violation and detected
contact as separate measurements. Stepping, downstream learning and hardware are
separate successors; do not add them as unsupported contribution claims or dilute
single-beam confirmation to meet a calendar target.
